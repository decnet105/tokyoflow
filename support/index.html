OFFICIAL TECHNICAL SUPPORT • TOKYOFLOW iOS & ACADEMY

How do I restore my purchases?

In TokyoFlow iOS, tap Settings (設定) → Restore Purchases. Your App Store receipt will be validated instantly without extra charges.

Why is there no sound during shadowing?

Ensure your iPhone physical Silent/Mute switch is turned off or check volume controls in Control Center. You can also test speech playback in the in-app audio settings.

Where is my study data stored?

TokyoFlow stores all bookmarks, diagnostic review schedules, and Tokyo Points locally on your device in secure sandbox storage for 100% privacy.
