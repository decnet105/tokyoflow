Frequently Asked Questions

1. How does the Karaoke Shadowing feature work?

TokyoFlow uses millisecond-level word timing synchronized with native audio. As the audio plays, each word or phrase highlights in real-time, helping you match authentic Japanese rhythm and pitch accent.

2. How do I switch between English and Chinese?

You can toggle between English and Chinese anytime by tapping the language button (🇺🇸 EN / 🇨🇳 中文) located in the top navigation bar or inside your Profile / Passport settings.

3. Can I practice with Romaji (romanized phonetic guide)?

Yes! Romaji can be toggled on or off at any time directly above Kanji words across all scenarios, dialogues, and vocabulary cards.

4. How does the Weakness Drill Lab work?

The Dojo and scenario tests automatically identify words or grammatical nuances you hesitate on. They are placed in your Weakness Drill Lab and dynamically graduate to "Mastered" as your recall speed and accuracy improve.

5. Is an internet connection required to study?

All core scenarios, dojo quizzes, audio assets, and shadowing tools are bundled locally in TokyoFlow for instant, reliable offline learning anytime, anywhere.
