Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Welcome to TokyoFlow ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

TokyoFlow is designed with a privacy-first architecture:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide personal credentials (email, name, phone number) to access core learning features.
  • Local Device Storage: Your study progress, Tokyo Points, streak counters, bookmarked words, and weak word diagnostics are stored locally on your device using on-device sandbox storage.
  • No Tracking: We do not track you across third-party apps or websites. We do not sell or share any user data with data brokers.

2. Microphone & Audio Playback

If you use pronunciation drills or shadowing features, audio is processed on-device. We do not record or upload your voice audio to external servers.

3. Third-Party Services

TokyoFlow integrates with official YouTube video lessons. When you tap to view videos, interactions comply with YouTube's standard Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Children's Privacy

TokyoFlow does not collect any personal identifiable information from children of any age.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at: support@tokyoflow.app
