THE TEXTBOOK ILLUSION
01

Decontextualized Translation

Translating arbitrary sentences fails completely in high-context Japanese culture (場 - Ba).

02

Ignored Linguistic Registers

Mixing honorifics (Keigo), polite form (Teineigo), and peer slang without context creates social awkwardness.

03

Flat, Robotic Synthesizers

Learning without pitch accent leaves learners with a flat foreign cadence that native Tokyoites struggle to decipher.
