## Description
TOKYO 8 PM HALF-PRICE HUNT
IN 30 SECONDS!
Japanese Phrase: 夜8時になると、お惣菜に半額シールが貼られます。 (Yoru hachi-ji ni naru to, osouzai ni hangaku shiiru ga hararemasu.)
English: At 8 PM, half-price stickers are placed on prepared food.
Rule: Look for the red '半額' (hangaku) sticker on fresh sushi and bento boxes after 8 PM!

VOCABULARY:
- 8時に (はちじに) : At 8 PM
- なると、 (なると) : When it turns,
- お惣菜に (おそうざいに) : On food
- 半額シールが (はんがくしーるが) : Half-price stickers
- 貼られます (はられます) : Are attached
