## Description
ONSEN SURVIVAL RULES
IN 30 SECONDS!
Japanese Phrase: 湯船に入る前に、体をきれいに洗ってください。 (Yubune ni hairu mae ni, karada o kirei ni aratte kudasai.)
English: Wash your body clean before getting into the bath.
Rule: Always wash and rinse your body thoroughly at the seated wash stalls before stepping into the tub!

VOCABULARY:
- 湯船に (ゆぶねに) : In the tub
- 入る前に、 (はいるまえに) : Before entering,
- 体を (からだを) : Body
- きれいに (きれいに) : Cleanly
- 洗ってください (あらってください) : Please wash
