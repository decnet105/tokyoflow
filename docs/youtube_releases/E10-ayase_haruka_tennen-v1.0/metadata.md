### KEY JAPANESE SENTENCE:
- 綾瀬はるかが、会場で天然発言をしました。
- (Ayase Haruka ga, kaijou de tennen hatsugen o shimashita.)
- Meaning: "Ayase Haruka made a spontaneous and charming comment at the venue."

### VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR BREAKDOWN:
1. 綾瀬はるか (Ayase Haruka) = Renowned Japanese Actress
2. 会場 (kaijou) = Venue / Press Hall
3. 天然 (tennen) = Natural / Uncalculated endearing charm
4. 発言 (hatsugen) = Remark / Statement
5. しました (shimashita) = Did / Made (Past polite form of the JLPT N5 verb 'suru')
