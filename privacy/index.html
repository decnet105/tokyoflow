Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 29, 2026
Privacy First Guarantee:

TokyoFlow is designed with privacy at its foundation. Your audio shadowing recordings and learning progress stay 100% on your personal device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or rent any of your personal data. Specifically:

  • No Account Required: You can use TokyoFlow immediately without registering an account, email, or password.
  • No Audio Uploads: When you use the microphone for shadowing practice and pronunciation feedback, your voice is processed entirely in real-time in memory and never uploaded to any remote server.
  • No Cross-App Tracking: We do not track you across third-party apps or websites, and we do not use IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers).

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a seamless learning experience, TokyoFlow stores the following data locally on your device via standard iOS storage (UserDefaults):

  • Selected display language (English / Simplified Chinese)
  • Completed learning packages and check-in streak status
  • Dojo battle scores, mastered items, and Weakness Lab drill history
  • Playback preferences (Romaji toggle, audio playback speed)

This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

3. Microphone Permission

TokyoFlow requests access to your device microphone strictly to enable shadowing recording features. Microphone data is analyzed exclusively on-device during your practice sessions.

4. Children's Privacy

TokyoFlow is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
