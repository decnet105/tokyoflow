Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: SEPTEMBER 2026 • COMPLIANT WITH APPLE PRIVACY GUIDELINES

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

TokyoFlow is designed from the ground up around on-device architectural privacy:

  • No Account Required: You do not need an account, email address, or third-party login to study Japanese.
  • Sandboxed Device Storage: Your study progress, saved flashcards, pitch accent bookmarks, and diagnostic weak spots remain 100% on your device.
  • Zero Tracking: We do not track you across third-party applications, sell information to data brokers, or run advertising trackers.

2. Audio Processing & Microphone Usage

When you practice shadowing or speech recognition exercises, all acoustic audio analysis is processed directly on your Apple device using native iOS Speech APIs. No voice recording data is uploaded to remote cloud servers.

3. Third-Party Integrations

TokyoFlow provides embedded links to official video lessons on our YouTube Academy. Interacting with YouTube links follows Google and YouTube standard Privacy Policies.

4. Inquiries & Support

For any privacy inquiries or feedback regarding our software architecture, please reach out to us at: support@tokyoflow.app
