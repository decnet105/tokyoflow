## 3. Hero Photography & Scenario Realism Directives (封面选图与场景真实合理性法则)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> 1. **Priority 1: Authentic HD Media Stills (真实高清原图优先)**:
>    - For entertainment, cinema, or news topics, prioritize authentic high-resolution frames or official press photos of the trending celebrity or scene (e.g. Hikari Mitsushima, Ayase Haruka, Tokyo landmark events).
> 2. **Priority 2: Scenario Realism & Common Sense (场景真实性与常识合规)**:
>    - When generating photorealistic base imagery without real stills, **the subject's attire, pose, and environment MUST strictly conform to real-world common sense** (例如：日本桑拿房必须符合现实规范使用桑拿浴巾，严禁出现穿洋装/裙子等脱离现实常识的违和设计)。
> 3. **Aesthetic Standard: Minimalist, Atmospheric & Grounded (简洁大气与高级质感)**:
>    - Prioritize clean composition, natural ambient cedar/neon lighting, genuine human expressions, and high immersion over artificial clutter. Keep the overall look professional, grounded, and unmistakably authentic to Tokyo.
